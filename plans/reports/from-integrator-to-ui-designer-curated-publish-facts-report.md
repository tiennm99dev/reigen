## 2. NEW SECTION — "Origin story" (before Personality)

Prose, no table. Facts to use:

- Worked an ordinary salaryman job at a regular company. Nothing to do with the paranormal.
- Quit out of boredom and dissatisfaction with the work.
- Opened Spirits and Such Consultation Office roughly four years before the story starts — renting the
  office essentially on impulse, without a real business plan.
- Mob turned up early on as a client, looking for help controlling powers he couldn't switch off.
  Reigen — who has no powers at all — took the job.

Framing worth writing toward: the business exists because a bored office worker walked out and rented a
room, and the single most consequential thing he ever did was answer the door to a kid he could not
possibly help in the way advertised.

**Do NOT publish:** childhood bullying, the school essay about changing the world, "various odd jobs
helping people" — all weak single-source.

## 5. RELATIONSHIPS — additions to existing entries

- **Serizawa Katsuya** (expand): hired after the final arc and made deputy director. Reigen cut his hair
  himself, a nudge toward independence. Reigen's habitual "Right, Serizawa?" is him fishing for backup.
- **Tome Kurata** (expand): never actually hired — she installed herself as unpaid secretary and simply
  kept turning up. Friendly needling by the end.
- **Tsubomi Takane** (NEW entry): came to the office as a client with a friend and tested his
  credibility with a trick question. Reigen noticed Mob's photo on his own "satisfied customers" board.
  No romantic angle — he simply respected the bond between her and Mob.
- **Dimple** (existing entry, adjust): the bickering "old married couple" dynamic is single-source —
  keep the existing wording, don't add it.
